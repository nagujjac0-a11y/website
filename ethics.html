Ethics

WHO is committed to the highest standards of integrity, professionalism and accountability. These ethical principles guide how WHO staff, partners and contractors conduct themselves in Uganda and everywhere WHO works.

Our Core Principles

Integrity

Acting honestly and transparently in all work and decisions.

Accountability

Taking responsibility for actions, resources and results.

Respect

Treating every person with dignity, regardless of background.

Independence

Making decisions based on evidence and public health needs.

Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse

WHO has zero tolerance for sexual exploitation, abuse and harassment. All personnel are expected to uphold these standards, and concerns can be reported through official channels.

Conflicts of Interest

WHO staff and experts must declare any interests that could affect their impartiality. This protects the credibility of WHO guidance and recommendations.

Research Ethics

Health research supported by WHO must respect participants’ rights, obtain informed consent and protect personal information.
