Cyber Security

Health information is sensitive, and WHO takes the protection of its digital systems and data seriously. This page outlines good practices that help keep information safe for staff, partners and visitors to this site.

Staying Safe Online

  • Use strong passwords: choose long, unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication where possible.
  • Beware of phishing: do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments, even if the message appears to come from WHO.
  • Keep software updated: install security updates on devices promptly.
  • Protect personal data: share health and personal information only through approved channels.
  • Use secure networks: avoid sensitive work on unsecured public Wi-Fi.

Reporting a Security Concern

If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from WHO or believe a security incident has occurred, please report it using the details on our Contact Us page.

Data Protection

Personal data is collected and used only for legitimate public health purposes and is handled in line with applicable data protection principles.
