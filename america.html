America

Health partnerships connect regions across the world.

WHO’s presence in the Americas is delivered through the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), which serves as the WHO Regional Office for the Americas, based in Washington, D.C., United States.

PAHO supports countries in North, Central and South America and the Caribbean in strengthening health systems, controlling disease and promoting health equity.

Regional Priorities

  • Primary health care and universal access to health services
  • Vaccination and elimination of vaccine-preventable diseases
  • Prevention of noncommunicable diseases such as diabetes and heart disease
  • Emergency preparedness for epidemics and natural disasters
  • Reducing health inequalities among communities

Learning Across Regions

Uganda and other African countries benefit from experiences shared by regions around the world. Lessons from immunization campaigns, surveillance systems and emergency response in the Americas help inform global health practice.
