Careers

Working with WHO Uganda means contributing to better health for people across the country. We welcome skilled, motivated people who share our commitment to public health.

Why Work With Us

Meaningful Impact

Support work that protects and improves the health of communities.

Professional Growth

Learn from experts and develop your career in global health.

Collaboration

Work alongside government, UN agencies and local partners.

Diversity

We value diverse backgrounds, skills and perspectives.

Areas of Work

  • Disease prevention and control
  • Emergency preparedness and response
  • Maternal and child health
  • Health systems strengthening
  • Administration, finance and communications
